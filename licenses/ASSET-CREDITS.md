# Asset credits and distribution notes

The anatomical base, selected original skin/eye/mouth geometry and texture inputs are from the MakeHuman community and MPFB. Selected system assets have explicit CC0 grants from Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier and other contributors. See MakeHuman-CC0.md and config/source-downloads.json for the pinned source and archive hashes.

Additional selected CC0 assets: Mindfront eyebrows 02 and eyelashes 01; MRT / Toigo basic tucked T-shirt and wool trousers; Joel Palmius female tee body-coverage mask; Mika Suominen ARKit face units. Original embedded headers were checked. See config/assets.json and reports/source-selection.md. CC0 does not legally require attribution, but credits are retained.

The MPFB tool code is GPL-3.0-or-later, distinct from its CC0 assets and output. The build fetches its exact source commit. Browser dependencies retain their own licenses in package-lock.json/node_modules and bundled notices as applicable.

The portrait reference images were supplied by the project owner in this repository. A color texture derived from those references remains a user-supplied derivative; no new independent license for the pictured likeness or source imagery is asserted. The owner explicitly authorized delivery of this project’s model, viewer and source to a new branch of 9r89uf8/avatar on 3 October 2026. This does not establish an independent third-party license for the supplied imagery.

The new strand groom, skin microrelief, clothing seam geometry, scripts and viewer are original work for this project. No paid or extractably restricted scan or human-library asset is included. The o4saken_long01 hair and other ambiguous pack assets are deliberately excluded from the build.
